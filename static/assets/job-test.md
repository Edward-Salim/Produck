About the job
YouTrip is the leading and fastest-growing multi-currency payment platform in Asia Pacific. In 2018, we pioneered the region's first multi-currency digital wallet. Now, YouTrip is the #1 multi-currency digital wallet trusted by millions across the region, processing over US$15 billion in total payment value annually. 

Our consumer and business propositions - YouTrip and YouBiz - empower individuals and businesses with inclusive, accessible, and affordable financial solutions. YouTrip has raised over US$110 million to date, including its recent Series B round led by global venture capital firm Lightspeed Venture Partners—underscoring strong investor confidence in its mission to transform digital financial services across the Asia Pacific. 

Recognised as World Economic Forum's Global Innovator 2025, CNBC's World's Top Fintech Companies (2025, 2024), and LinkedIn's Top Startup (2025, 2024, 2023), YouTrip is dedicated to creating the next generation of digital finance services for consumers and businesses.
Valuing an entrepreneurial culture of diversity and inclusion, we are seeking driven individuals to join our fast-growing regional team as we push the boundaries of innovative fintech solutions.

We are looking for a Product Manager to join YouTrip's Product team, working on our core platform that powers critical user, transaction, and operational capabilities. This role is ideal for a technically curious Product Manager with 1-4 years of experience who enjoys working on complex systems and wants to understand how a fintech product operates end-to-end.You will work on one of our core problem spaces at a time, owning meaningful product areas while developing a strong understanding of how our platform, internal teams, and external partners work together. The scope spans user-facing experiences, internal tools, and backend systems that must be accurate, reliable, and scalable.This role reports into the Director of Product Management and works closely with engineering, operations, finance, risk, compliance, and customer support teams.


What you'll do

Own and drive product initiatives within a core platform area, from problem definition through delivery and iteration
Translate business, operational, and regulatory needs into clear product requirements and system workflows
Work closely with engineers on backend-heavy systems, integrations, APIs, data flows, and edge cases
Improve system reliability, accuracy, and scalability across transaction flows, internal processes, and controls
Partner with internal teams to reduce manual work, operational risk, and customer-impacting issues
Support integrations with external partners and vendors, ensuring clear ownership and expectations
Contribute to roadmap planning, backlog prioritisation, and sequencing with awareness of dependencies and trade-offs
Define and track product and operational metrics such as system accuracy, turnaround time, failure rates, and user impact
Produce clear documentation including product requirements, workflows, and system diagrams

Requirements

1-4 years of experience in product management, business analysis, or a closely related role
Comfortable working on technically complex or backend-heavy products
Strong ability to understand workflows that cut across engineering, operations, finance, and risk
Curious, fast learner who is not intimidated by ambiguity, constraints, or regulated environments
Able to work closely with engineers and ask thoughtful questions about system behaviour, data, and failure modes
Structured thinker who can break down messy problems into practical, executable solutions
Clear written communicator who values good documentation and clarity
Collaborative, low-ego team player who takes ownership and follows through
Bonus (not required)

Exposure to fintech, payments, financial systems, or regulated products
Experience with internal tooling, operational platforms, or system integrations
Familiarity with risk, compliance, fraud, or control-related product areas
Technical background or strong comfort working with backend systems

Why join YouTrip

Work on core systems that directly impact users, money movement, and platform trust
Gain broad exposure across critical product areas early in your career
Learn how strong product teams operate in a fast-scaling fintech
High ownership, real-world impact, and close collaboration with senior product and engineering leaders
Opportunity to deepen into a specific core area over time as your strengths develop
